# Quinte Mesh

**A Community Mesh Network for Belleville, Trenton, Picton, and Beyond**

---

### The Network is Live

We've got **7 MeshCore nodes** up and running across the Quinte region. The network is growing with the support of a wonderful community.

Mesh networks don't depend on cell towers or internet infrastructure. When the power goes out or the towers get overloaded, the mesh still works. It's a "Plan B" for the Quinte region, and it's free to use.

### How it Helps Our Region

* **Safety for Families:** Send off-grid text messages to loved ones during winter ice storms or in rural dead zones.
* **Rural Support:** Farmers can monitor livestock or soil sensors across large acreages without monthly cellular fees.
* **Municipal Resilience:** A backup communication layer for local towns and emergency teams during natural disasters.
* **Open Access:** Once the hardware is set up, the network is free to use and belongs to the community.

### Network Settings

We use the same MeshCore frequency settings as [Ottawa](https://ottawamesh.ca/) and [Toronto](https://meshcore.ca/), so all our nodes are compatible. The long term vision is a **Quebec City to Windsor corridor**, and Quinte is right in the middle of that.

<table class="settings-table">
  <tr><th>Setting</th><th>Value</th></tr>
  <tr><td>Frequency</td><td>910.525 MHz</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Bandwidth</td><td>62.5 kHz</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Spreading Factor</td><td>7</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Coding Rate</td><td>5</td></tr>
</table>

For setup guides and hardware recommendations, check out [OttawaMesh.ca](https://ottawamesh.ca/). We follow the same configuration.

### Get Involved

You don't need to be technical to help out. We're looking for:

* **Users:** Anyone who wants to use the network.
* **Hosts:** People willing to put a small node in their window or on their roof.
* **Collaborators:** Local businesses, farmers, and hobbyists with ideas for the network.
* **Organizers:** Anyone interested in helping with outreach and strategy.

Come say hi in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/FdBbEZnQ) and we'll help you get started.
