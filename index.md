# Quinte Mesh

**A Community Network for Belleville, Trenton, Picton, and Beyond**

### **What is Quinte Mesh?**

We are building a decentralized, community-owned communication network. Unlike cell service or home internet, a mesh network is powered by small, low-cost devices hosted by people like you. It creates a "Plan B" for the Quinte region that works even when the power is out or cell towers are overloaded.

### **How it Helps Our Region**

* **Safety for Families:** Send off-grid text messages to loved ones during winter ice storms or in rural dead zones.
* **Rural Support:** Farmers can monitor livestock or soil sensors across large acreages without monthly cellular fees.
* **Municipal Resilience:** Provides a backup communication layer for local towns and emergency teams during natural disasters.
* **Open Access:** Once the hardware is set up, the network is free to use and belongs to the community.

### **Get Involved**

You do not need to be a technical expert to help. We are looking for:

* **Users:** Anyone who wants to utilize this free network to connect.
* **Hosts:** People willing to place a small node in their window or on their roof.
* **Collaborators:** Local businesses, farmers, and hobbyists with ideas for the network.
* **Organizers:** Anyone interested in helping with outreach and strategy.
