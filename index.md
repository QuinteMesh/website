# Quinte Mesh

### A Community Network for Belleville, Trenton, Picton & Beyond

Our mission is to build a resilient, decentralized mesh network accessible to the 100,000+ residents of the Quinte region.

### Goals

* **Emergency Resilience:** Reliable communication when traditional infrastructure fails.
* **Local Connection:** Serving residents, businesses, and municipal governments.
* **Education:** Providing hands-on technology learning opportunities.
